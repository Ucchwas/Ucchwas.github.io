I’m a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Radiation Oncology at Stanford University, working with Prof. Md Tauhidul Islam on machine learning for biomedicine. I recently built BioGlyph, which turns network structure into language that frozen LLMs can reason over, and I’m now pretraining masked-autoencoder foundation models for proteomics.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Texas Tech University, where I was advised by Prof. Bashir I. Morshed and built real-time biosignal AI, from on-device arrhythmia detection to LLM-conditioned silent speech recognition. Before that, I earned a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BUET and was a software engineer at Fiftytwo (Bording Group), writing C/C++ for a retail point-of-sale platform. Outside of work, I play cricket and soccer.
