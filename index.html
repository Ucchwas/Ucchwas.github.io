About

I’m a Postdoctoral Scholar in Stanford University’s Department of Radiation Oncology, where I work on machine learning for biomedicine. My research focuses on foundation models, graph learning, multimodal biomedical data, and AI systems that can reason over complex scientific information. My goal is to build scalable, reliable ML methods that move beyond benchmarks and can support real-world scientific and clinical applications.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Texas Tech University, where I worked on real-time biosignal AI, from on-device arrhythmia detection to LLM-conditioned silent speech recognition. Before that, I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BUET and worked as a software engineer at Fiftytwo (Bording Group), building C/C++ systems for retail point-of-sale platforms. Outside of work, I love playing cricket and soccer.
